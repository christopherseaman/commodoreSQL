## 1. Decisions

### #42 — supply FormatType coverage
- Headline coverage/gap-share scoped to **2024+** (matches existing coverage cards 50/83); the
  **top-100 enrichment list** scoped to **Fall 2025** (matches #37). Empty FormatType = NULL or `''`.
- Top-100 ranked by **distinct section-adoption count** (breadth), placeholders left in for human triage.

### #44 — required vs supplemental pricing
- **Same-item demeaned method** (from card 161): subtract each ISBN's own median `price_min`, so the
  contrast is within-item; grouped by **required-status** (not control×level). ISBNs need ≥10
  adoptions spanning both statuses.
- **Primary flag = `is_required_inferred`**; a **literal `book_status`** cut (required vs
  option/recommended) added as robustness — re-sourced from `comprehensive_data` (not
  `pricing_historical`) to avoid a Metabase DuckDB-driver quirk on that join.

### #45 — Master ISBN variability
- **Reframed by the data:** ISBN13 is 1:1 with Title/Author/Format/FormatType/Publisher/Imprint, so
  an ISBN13-keyed "blessed value" correction is a **no-op**. The variability worth acting on is
  **title → many ISBN13s**; the proposed mechanism is a **blessed ISBN13 per title-cluster**
  (most-adopted), gated on author-diversity, for grad-student review. Builds on #37 (doesn't rebuild it).

### #46 — no price choice
- **Definition = `price_min = price_max`** (regardless of format) — every acquisition option costs
  the same, so there's no *cheaper* choice. Refined from an earlier `format_count ≤ 1` cut (kept as a
  secondary comparison column). A section counts if it has ≥1 priced required material **and all** of
  them are fixed-price. Sections with **no priced required material** are a **separate bucket** (not
  conflated with "one fixed price").

### #49 — pricing-index corruption
- Build the three `pricing_historical` indexes **after the last write** (end of `2b_`), not in `1_`
  before the `1b_`/`2b_` UPDATEs — a DuckDB ART index created before a large UPDATE gets corrupted for
  `=` point lookups.

### Deferral (per the call)

  mapping** (neither exists yet); #47 was explicitly "not for now."

## 3. Points of note & review

- **#42** — supplies are <2% of the FormatType gap, but **149 of 2,520 supply ISBNs (5.9%) DO carry a
  FormatType** (mostly Book/Bundle — lab/art kits bundled with a textbook; candidate for a classifier
  precision spot-check). The remaining gap (~167k non-supply ISBNs in Fall 2025) is the real enrichment
  opportunity.
- **#44** — the same book costs the same whether listed required or optional; the visible ~20% gap is
  **which books get flagged required** (course-mix), echoing the institution-level finding (card 161).
  Institution/program confounds aren't controlled (deep-dive).
- **#45** — the title clusters **mix genuinely distinct products under a generic title**
  (e.g. `macroeconomics` = 100 ISBNs / 48 authors) with boilerplate placeholders
  (`access in blackboard` = 137 ISBNs). **Not auto-collapsible** — needs human review; ~49% of
  clusters share one author (the safest same-book subset).
- **#46** — **definition needs your sign-off.** Current: min==max, all required priced materials. If
  you meant "*any* required item fixed-price" (rather than all), it's a one-line swap. The 79.5%
  "no priced required material at all" bucket dominates and is reported separately.
- **#49** — the bug was **latent**: no Metabase card or pipeline step used a bare `=` on the affected
  columns (all use joins/`IN`), so **no results were ever wrong**; the live DB is repaired and the
  pipeline hardened against recurrence.
